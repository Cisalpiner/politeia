og:card rasteriser

Crawlers do not render SVG, so the link-preview image has to be a PNG. This page draws docs/og-card.svg onto a canvas at 1200×630 and offers it as a download. There is no build step and no dependency — the browser is the renderer.

To regenerate after editing the SVG: open this page, click the button, and save the result as og-card.png in the repository root.
